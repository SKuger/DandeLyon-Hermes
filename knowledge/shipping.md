# Shipping

Orders placed before 2pm on a business day are dispatched the same day.
Orders placed after 2pm, on weekends or on a public holiday are dispatched
the next business day.

Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2
business days and costs 8 USD. Express is not available for PO boxes.

A tracking number is emailed when the parcel leaves the warehouse. Tracking
can take up to 24 hours to show movement, which is normal and does not mean
the parcel is lost.

We ship to the United States, Canada, Mexico, Colombia, Chile and Argentina.
We do not currently ship anywhere else.

# Returns and refunds

Unopened items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
Opened items can be returned within 14 days, and the refund is reduced by a
15 percent restocking fee.

Sale items and gift cards cannot be returned.

To start a return, reply with the order number and the reason. A prepaid
return label is emailed within one business day.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method and appear within 5 to 10
business days once the warehouse has received the item. We cannot refund to
a different card than the one used for the purchase.

A damaged item is not a return: send a photo and a replacement is shipped
immediately at no cost, with no need to send the damaged one back.
